# Cell Press Style Reference

This reference supports the `venue-templates` skill for Cell Press journals.

Always verify the current journal-specific guide for authors.

---

## 1. Scope

Use for journals under Cell Press or similar life-science/interdisciplinary formats.

For ordinary AI/CV method papers, use this only if the target journal is relevant.

---

## 2. Common Requirements to Check

Depending on journal and article type, check:

- article type;
- word limit;
- summary/abstract format;
- highlights;
- eTOC blurb;
- graphical abstract;
- main figure limits;
- STAR Methods or methods structure;
- resource availability;
- data/code availability;
- lead contact;
- author contributions;
- declaration of interests;
- supplemental information.

---

## 3. Graphical Abstracts and Highlights

Some Cell Press journals require:

- graphical abstract;
- highlights;
- eTOC blurb.

Venue Templates may identify this requirement.

Use:

- `scientific-schematics` for technical graphical abstracts or workflows;
- `generate-image` for non-technical visuals only when appropriate.

Do not generate graphical abstracts by default.

---

## 4. Methods and Resource Availability

Check whether the journal requires:

- STAR Methods;
- key resources table;
- data/code availability;
- experimental model details;
- statistical analysis details;
- resource identifiers.

Only include accurate information.

Do not fabricate resource availability or data/code statements.

---

## 5. Boundary

This reference is domain-specific.

Do not apply Cell Press expectations to AI/CV conference papers unless explicitly requested.

---

## 6. Checklist

- [ ] Exact Cell Press journal identified.
- [ ] Article type identified.
- [ ] Official guide verified.
- [ ] Highlights/eTOC/graphical abstract requirements checked.
- [ ] Methods/resource sections checked.
- [ ] Data/code availability checked.
- [ ] Visual needs routed appropriately.

---

## Final Reminder

Cell Press formatting can be highly specific.

Verify the exact journal instructions before applying requirements.
