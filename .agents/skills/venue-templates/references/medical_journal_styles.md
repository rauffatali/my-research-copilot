# Medical Journal Style Reference

This reference supports the `venue-templates` skill only for medical, clinical, biomedical, or health-related manuscripts.

Do not use this as the default for AI/CV method papers.

Always verify current journal and reporting guideline requirements.

---

## 1. Scope

Use when the target manuscript involves:

- clinical study;
- biomedical research;
- medical AI;
- diagnostic/prognostic models;
- patient data;
- clinical trial;
- observational health study;
- systematic review or meta-analysis.

For general AI/CV papers, use ML/CS conference or general journal references instead.

---

## 2. Common Medical Journal Requirements

Check:

- structured abstract;
- ethics approval;
- informed consent;
- clinical trial registration;
- data sharing statement;
- funding;
- conflict of interest;
- author contributions;
- reporting guideline checklist;
- patient/public involvement if relevant.

---

## 3. Reporting Guidelines

Use only when applicable:

- CONSORT for randomized clinical trials;
- STROBE for observational studies;
- PRISMA for systematic reviews/meta-analyses;
- TRIPOD for prediction models;
- CLAIM or DECIDE-AI for medical AI where relevant.

Do not force these onto non-clinical AI/CV manuscripts.

---

## 4. Medical AI Considerations

For medical AI papers, check:

- dataset source;
- patient-level split;
- external validation;
- bias/fairness;
- demographic reporting;
- clinical relevance;
- regulatory/deployment caveats;
- interpretability needs;
- data privacy.

Use `claim-auditor` for clinical claims.

---

## 5. Boundary

Medical formatting and reporting are high-stakes.

If the paper is clinical or medical, official journal instructions and reporting guidelines must be verified.

---

## 6. Checklist

- [ ] Medical/clinical relevance confirmed.
- [ ] Journal instructions verified.
- [ ] Reporting guideline identified.
- [ ] Ethics/consent requirements checked.
- [ ] Data sharing requirements checked.
- [ ] Clinical claims audited.
- [ ] Citation/reference integrity routed to `citation-management`.

---

## Final Reminder

Medical journal requirements are not generic science formatting.

Use them only when the manuscript type requires them.
