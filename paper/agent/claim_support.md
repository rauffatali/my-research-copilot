# Claim Support

## Claim Support Rule

Use `docs/agent/claim_ledger.md` as the source of truth for support status.

Do not introduce different support labels in this file.

Every manuscript-facing claim should map to one of:

- `supported`
- `partially_supported`
- `preliminary`
- `hypothesis`
- `needs_citation`
- `wrong_citation`
- `unsupported`
- `contradicted`
- `out_of_scope`
- `needs_manual_check`

## Status
- draft
