## Closure Status Values

Use one of these revision-closure statuses:

- `open`
- `in_progress`
- `verified`
- `accepted_risk`
- `deferred`

These are revision workflow statuses, not claim support statuses.

For each revision item:

- `Originating Finding` must link to the reviewer finding or issue that caused the revision.
- `Affected Claim / Section` must identify the manuscript claim or section being changed.
- `Evidence References` must identify the durable evidence or explicitly recorded missing evidence relevant to the revision.
- `Closure Status` must show whether the item is open, in progress, verified, accepted as risk, or deferred.
- `Closure Verification` must state how the change was checked, or why an accepted risk or deferral is justified.
